Our Story

Founded in 2018, Thrive with Sharuja began in a small community center with a single mission: to provide the kind of care that treats the child, not just the diagnosis. Sharuja recognized that traditional pediatric therapy often lacked the emotional intelligence and family-centered focus needed for true, sustainable growth.

What started as one therapist's vision has blossomed into a multidisciplinary sanctuary where hundreds of families find hope and tangible progress every year.
