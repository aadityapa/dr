# ₹100 Crore Roadmap — Thrive with Sharuja

Honest framing first: a single-therapist clinic website cannot generate ₹100Cr. A **brand + system built around Dr. Sharuja** can. One clinic doing very well earns ₹1–3Cr/year. ₹100Cr valuation/revenue means turning what works in Kandivali into products, locations, and licensing. The website is the engine that makes each stage possible.

## Stage 1 (Months 0–6): Own Kandivali & Mumbai — ₹0 → ₹1.5Cr/yr
- **Google Business Profile is worth more than any ad.** Get 150+ reviews (ask every happy parent, QR card at reception). Respond to all reviews. This alone decides who ranks #1 for "occupational therapist near me".
- **Local SEO pages you already have — push harder:** one landing page per suburb (Kandivali, Borivali, Malad, Goregaon…) with real testimonials from that area. The `/conditions/*` and short URLs (`/autism`, `/adhd`) are already good — build backlinks to them from parenting blogs, school newsletters, Practo/Justdial profiles.
- **Convert better, not just more traffic:** WhatsApp is your #1 booking channel in India — track clicks on the floating button. Add online payment + slot booking (Cal.com/Razorpay) so a parent can book at 11pm when they're worried.
- **Capture every visitor:** the free screening tool + library downloads are lead magnets. Follow up every lead within 24h — a simple CRM (HubSpot free) doubles conversion.
- KPI: 100+ new patient enquiries/month from the site.

## Stage 2 (Months 6–18): Become the trusted name online — ₹1.5Cr → ₹5Cr/yr
- **YouTube + Instagram in Hindi/Marathi/English** (your site is already trilingual — that's rare and a real edge). 2 videos/week: "Is my child just naughty or is it sensory?" style content. Parents search in these languages; almost no doctor answers them well.
- **Paid course for parents** (₹2,000–5,000): home therapy programs for autism/ADHD/sensory issues. Recorded once, sold forever. 1,000 sales/yr = ₹30–50L with ~90% margin.
- **Teletherapy + parent coaching subscriptions** (₹3–8k/month) — serves all of India, not just Kandivali.
- **Hire 2–4 therapists** under the brand; Dr. Sharuja moves to assessments + supervision. Website books for the team, not one person.
- KPI: 50k+ followers, ₹40L+/yr from digital products.

## Stage 3 (Years 2–4): Multiply — ₹5Cr → ₹25Cr/yr
- **3–5 owned centres** in Mumbai/Pune/Thane. The website becomes a multi-location platform (each location page already exists in your code structure).
- **Therapist training academy**: certify OTs in your method (₹50k–1L per certification). This creates your franchise talent pipeline and authority.
- **School partnerships**: screening programs for preschools — B2B contracts of ₹2–5L/school/year, plus a referral pipeline.
- KPI: 5 centres, 500 certifications/yr, 20 school contracts.

## Stage 4 (Years 4–7): Platform — ₹25Cr → ₹100Cr
- **Franchise/license 30–50 centres** across India under the brand (India has a massive shortage of pediatric OT; demand far exceeds supply). Franchise fee + royalty model.
- **App/SaaS**: home-program app for parents (subscription) + practice software for franchisees.
- **Raise funding at this stage, not earlier** — healthcare chains with proven unit economics get strong valuations; ₹100Cr valuation needs roughly ₹15–25Cr revenue with good margins.
