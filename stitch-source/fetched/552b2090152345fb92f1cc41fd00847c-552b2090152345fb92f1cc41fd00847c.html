Common Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries about our therapy and consultation process.

How do I book an initial consultation?

expand_more
You can book directly through our website using the "Book Now" button, or fill out the contact form above. Our intake specialist will contact you within one business day to discuss your needs and schedule an appointment.

Do you accept insurance?

expand_more
Yes, we work with several major insurance providers. Please reach out to our administration team with your policy details, and we'll help verify your coverage before your first visit.

What ages of children do you work with?

expand_more
Our specialized pediatric team works with infants through adolescents, typically ranging from birth to 18 years of age. We tailor our approach to each developmental stage.
